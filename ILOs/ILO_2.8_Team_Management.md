# ILO 2.8: Emotional Self-Management and Team Support

**Requirement**: Ability to recognise and manage your own emotions and those of others, make decisions that further project and team outcomes.

## Evidence in the Project

### 1. Agile Scrum Methodology
The team follows the Agile Scrum framework, which inherently requires strong communication and team support:
- **Sprint Planning**: Team members collaborate to estimate effort and assign tasks using Azure Boards.
- **Daily Stand-ups (Implicit)**: Regular communication to block out issues and support team members who are stuck.

### 2. Sprint Retrospectives
- Retrospectives are conducted at the end of each sprint using the Azure DevOps Retrospectives plugin.
- This provides a structured environment for the team to reflect on what went well, what didn't, and how processes can be improved. It is a key mechanism for emotional management and resolving conflicts or frustrations in a constructive manner.

### 3. Peer Reviews and Constructive Feedback
- Pull Requests (PRs) on GitHub require peer review before merging. This process encourages constructive criticism, technical discussion, and mutual support to ensure everyone's code meets the project's high standards.

### Summary
While the direct evidence for emotional self-management lies in individual learning logs and peer review forms, the project structure—specifically the use of structured PRs, Agile ceremonies, and collaborative troubleshooting—creates an environment that necessitates and supports these skills.
